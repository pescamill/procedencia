Looting · 25 December 1985

The robbery of the National Museum of Anthropology

In the early hours of Christmas 1985, two students got in through the air-conditioning ducts and took 124 pieces from the Maya, Mixtec and Mexica halls. In 1989 the police recovered 111. The rest have yet to return.

National Museum of Anthropology, Mexico City

What the sources show

  • On 25 December 1985, 124 pieces were stolen, among them the Zapotec bat-god mask, objects from the sacred cenote of Chichén Itzá and Mixtec gold jewelry. [1, 2]
  • On 10 June 1989, 111 pieces were recovered in a house in Ciudad Satélite; the rest have not fully returned to the museum. [1]

Sources
