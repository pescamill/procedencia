Rubber, 3,400 years before Goodyear

The peoples of the Gulf of Mexico mixed latex with the juice of a vine to make durable rubber from around 1600 BCE. When Charles Goodyear patented vulcanization in 1844, industrial history told it as the beginning of useful rubber and left out those who were already processing it.

El Manatí, Veracruz → United States

What the sources show

  • From around 1600 BCE, Castilla elastica latex was mixed with Ipomoea alba juice to obtain rubber with different properties. [1]
  • By varying the ratio they obtained balls that bounce, wear-resistant soles or adhesive bands: applied polymer chemistry. [2]
  • In 1844 Goodyear patented the treatment of rubber with sulfur and heat. [3]
