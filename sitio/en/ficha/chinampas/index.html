Agriculture

Chinampas

Its original name:

  • chinamitl (Nahuatl): reed enclosure [1]

Hand-built farming islands on the lakes of the Valley of Mexico, among the most productive farmland ever known. They are still worked in Xochimilco.

Verdict

Plots built on the lake, extremely productive. In 2017 the FAO recognized them as a Globally Important Agricultural Heritage System. [2, 3] · Consensus

What the sources show

  • They supplied a large share of Tenochtitlan's food. [3]
  • There were extensive chinampas before the Mexica: those of Xaltocan, in the north of the basin, were abandoned in the 14th century. [4]
