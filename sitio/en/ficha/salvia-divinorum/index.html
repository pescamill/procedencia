Salvia divinorum

Its original name:

  • ska pastora (Mazatec)

A sacred plant of the Mazatec; its main compound is studied in neuroscience.

Verdict

The Mazatec ritual plant is the source of salvinorin A, the first known natural kappa-opioid agonist without nitrogen. [1, 2] · Consensus

See also

  • Sacred mushrooms and psilocybin

Sources

  1. Ortega, A.; Blount, J. F.; Manchand, P. S. (1982). Salvinorin, a new trans-neoclerodane diterpene from Salvia divinorum (Labiatae). Journal of the Chemical Society, Perkin Transactions 1.
  2. Roth, B. L.; Baner, K.; Westkaemper, R. et al. (2002). Salvinorin A: a potent naturally occurring nonnitrogenous κ opioid selective agonist. PNAS 99(18).
