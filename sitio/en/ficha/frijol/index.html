Food

Common bean

Its original name:

  • etl (Nahuatl) [1]
  • búul (Yucatec Maya) [2]

Black, pinto, red and white beans, and green beans too, are a single species domesticated in Mexico.

Verdict

The common bean was domesticated twice, separately: once in Mesoamerica and once in the Andes. [3] · Consensus

What the sources show

  • The bean reference genome shows two independent domestications, with different genes selected in each. [3]
  • The Mesoamerican center of domestication lies in the Lerma–Santiago basin, in western Mexico. [4]
  • Other bean species were also domesticated in Mesoamerica, such as the runner bean (ayocote) and the tepary bean. [5]
