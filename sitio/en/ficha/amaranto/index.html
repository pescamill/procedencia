Food

Amaranth

Its original name:

  • huauhtli (Nahuatl) [1]

The seed of alegría; the Mexica used it in ceremonies, and today it is sold worldwide as a superfood.

Verdict

Amaranthus hypochondriacus and A. cruentus were domesticated in Mesoamerica; the Andean species, A. caudatus, has a different history. [2, 3] · Supported

What the sources show

  • Genomics shows several attempts to domesticate amaranth in the Americas, with parallel adaptations in seed color. [3]
  • After the conquest its cultivation declined because of its use in Mexica ceremonies. [2]
